## Candidates generated and killed (7)

1. **FAQPage JSON-LD on `/faq`** (Track B) — `app/(customer)/faq/page.tsx`
   renders `FAQS` from `lib/content/defaults.ts` (plain `{q, a}` strings, no
   JSON-LD anywhere) and looked like a clean follow-on to CC-004's now-merged
   `Service` schema. **Killed by the research itself**: Google restricted
   FAQPage rich results to government/health sites in 2023, "totally
   devalued" the feature in May 2026, and a March 2026 update "demoted
   FAQPage schema on non-FAQ content at scale." Shipping this now would apply
   guidance that's actively wrong for a commercial marketplace site.
2. **Convert `<img>` to `next/image`** (Track B) — 13 files use raw `<img>`
   (avatars, job photos, provider cards). Next.js 16 requires `remotePatterns`
   in `next.config.ts` (not present) plus, per current guidance, a custom
   Supabase-transform loader to avoid `INVALID_IMAGE_OPTIMIZE_REQUEST`; several
   usages have focal-point cropping (`object-position` driven by
   `avatar_focal_x/y`) that would need re-verifying under `next/image`. Real,
   but config + 13 call sites + visual re-testing is well past a day — M/L,
   not the S this run is looking for.
3. **Dispute response-time SLA copy** (Track A) — research: explicit
   response-time commitments ("respond within 72 hours") measurably build
   trust in dispute flows. Checked `admin/disputes/page.tsx` and
   `booking-copy.ts`'s dispute section: no such number exists anywhere in the
   codebase, only "a founder reviews every dispute personally." Surfacing a
   number here means inventing an operational promise, not moving an
   existing fact — a business decision, same category `decisions.md` already
   defers until there's real data.
4. **Per-provider "typical response time" stat** (Track A) — checked whether
   `RESPONSE_WINDOW_HOURS` / `response_window_hours` was a computed provider
   average; it's actually a customer-chosen alert deadline set per booking
   request, not a historical stat. Building the real thing needs new
   aggregation against a 6-provider sample too thin to be meaningful.
5. **Public on-time/completion-rate badge** (Track A) — no such aggregate
   exists in `lib/db/types.ts` or any migration. New feature, and again the
   6-provider pilot sample is too thin for the number to mean anything.
6. **WCAG required-field indicator audit** (Track B) — checked
   `provider-settings-forms.tsx`, `account-forms.tsx`, `signup-form.tsx`.
   The codebase already has a working, consistent convention — optional
   fields are labeled inline ("Company name (optional)", "Greek house or
   chapter (optional)"), same pattern the review form already uses
   ("Written review (optional)"). Went in expecting a gap like CC-007's; it
   isn't one.
7. **.edu student-verification trust badge** (Track A) — already built:
   `SchoolIdentity` shows the school logo/name (verified via `.edu` OTP at
   onboarding) on both Browse cards and profile pages.

No candidate survived. Per the prompt's own instruction ("if your research
produced neither, say so rather than inventing one") — Track A's strongest
finding (dispute-SLA transparency) required inventing a business commitment,
and Track B's cleanest-looking win (FAQPage schema) was contradicted by its
own research. Better to report zero than pad the queue with either.
